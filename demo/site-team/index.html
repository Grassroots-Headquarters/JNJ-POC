Site App demos · Site team · not the real app

Which Site team app would the lads actually use?

Three different layouts of the same flow: pick a job, sign in on site, request materials, add a photo, flag an issue, message the team. Switch with the pill at the bottom or the arrow keys. Nothing is saved or sent.

What just happened

The three layouts

A · Tiles. The current wireframes. Job home with four big tiles and a sign-in banner.
B · Chat-first. Opens like a WhatsApp group. Materials, photos and issues are buttons above the message box and post into the thread as cards.
C · Action-first. No job list. One big sign-in button for today's job, then four big actions. Change job with the chip at the top.
